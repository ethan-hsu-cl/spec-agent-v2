# Observations — R2 (New Year rows, Android 16.9.0 2611231)

- **O-1 (TC-003, minor).** At some launches right after a phone date change (12/28 00:00, 1/5 00:03, 1/5 23:58) a toast 「PiP video was enabled before.」 showed over the interstitial; logcat `V pwe : PiP video was enabled before.` from the app's process. No spec mentions it. Evidence: screenshots/TC-003-02.png.
- **O-2 (TC-003 / TC-020, pricing).** On the US store the interstitial reads 「SAVE 52%」 「vs. paying $7.99/mo × 12」 (regular monthly SKU's store price) while the IAP page's campaign card reads 「45% OFF」 with 「$6.99/Month」 (campaign monthly). The two campaign screens disagree on the discount. Evidence: TC-020-01.png, TC-003-05.png.
- **O-3 (TC-026 / TC-003, countdown).** The New Year IAP page's 「Continue」 carries a countdown (「7D 12:09:11」 on 12/29 11:50, 「8D 23:59:04」 on 12/28 00:00) and the Launcher pill alternates between 「On Sale!」 and a countdown (「6D 12:02:17」); both run to the end of 1/5. Spec check of the countdown is in R2-c rows.
- **O-4 (once a day vs date travel).** After the phone date went back from 2027-01-06 to 2026-12-29 the interstitial showed again on 12/29, although it had already been shown on 12/29 at 11:49 (the once-a-day memory seems to keep only the last day shown). Artifact of moving the clock backwards; noted for R2-b/c, which start with it showing.
- **O-5 (script / phone).** Setting the date in Settings makes a Samsung 「Software update / Update ready to install」 screen (com.wssyncmldm) come up now and then; closed with Back, never installed. `cmd alarm set-time` does not change the clock (no permission). log-slice windows are host time while the phone's clock is shifted 34+ days, so by-case excerpts are empty: log causes were read from logs/R2-a-*/logcat-live.txt by phone time. The first `id tv_more_subscription_plans` tap did nothing (tap the area 540,2073).
- **O-6 (R2-b, New Year title art and CTA per language).** Beyond the four English strings every non-English language keeps (R1 O-7: 「SAVE」, 「vs. paying NT$240/mo × 12」, 「/year」, 「More subscription plan」; Arabic / Persian digits in 「× 12」 again), the New Year interstitial differs from the 多國文案 sheet where the Christmas one did not: the title art is the English 「New Year Special」 in French (TC-059, sheet 「Vente du Nouvel An」) and Italian (TC-063, 「Capodanno Speciale」); Ukrainian shows the Russian art 「Новогодняя акция」 (TC-082, sheet 「Новорічна пропозиція」); es-ES shows the ESP (MX) title 「Especial de Año Nuevo」 and CTA 「Prueba gratis por 7 días」 (TC-066) while es-MX's CTA is 「Inicia tu prueba gratuita de 7 días」 (TC-071). CTA wording differs from the sheet in CHT, CHS, FRA, ITA, JPN, KOR, ESP, ESP (MX), RUS, Viet, Arabic, Hebrew (same set as R1-b); bottom-bar overlap in RUS, touching separators in FRA, UKR, Polish; Polish CTA wraps to two lines edge to edge. Titles in all other languages match the sheet. Evidence screenshots/TC-0xx-01.png.
- **O-7 (R2-b, script).** The once-a-day interstitial was brought back for each language by moving the phone date between 12/29 and 12/30 (Settings › Date and time › Set date), no pm clear (KP-093 keeps the account signed in). After every move to 12/30 the Samsung 「Software update」 screen came up within ~3 s; when it comes up after the launch it sits over the interstitial, and a Back meant for it closes the interstitial (TC-060 first try, TC-057 first try). Wait ~3 s after the date change, close the update screen, then launch.
